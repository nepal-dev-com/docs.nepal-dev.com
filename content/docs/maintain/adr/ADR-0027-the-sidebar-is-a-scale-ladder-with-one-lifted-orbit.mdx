## Context

The owner reported on 2026-11-01 that the docs sidebar does not feel good: "so many card like
view, while overview and maintainer documentation is small". The hierarchy should be rethought
now that the content is known.

What the sidebar shows today (ADR-0018 decision 2, ADR-0023, ADR-0025;
`src/components/docs/orbit-station.tsx`, `src/app/site.css`):

- **Every folder is a card**, open or closed: a bordered panel with a glyph, the name, a count
  pill and a chevron. This site's Maintain track has 12 folders holding 0 to 25 pages, so its
  sidebar is a column of 12 equal cards, and a folder with one page weighs as much as the ADRs.
- **Top-level pages are the smallest rows.** *Overview* and *Maintainer documentation*, the
  two entry points, are 13.5px muted rows on a short arc above the cards.
- **Nothing groups the folders.** Fumadocs separators (`---Label---` in a `meta.json`) render
  as a dotted rule, but no track uses them.
- The content is now known: a package's Use line holds a few pages and a few folders (getting
  started, guides, reference); a project of several repositories adds a folder per repository
  (ADR-0024); this site's Maintain holds 2 pages and 12 folders, the ADRs among them.

The owner chose on 2026-11-01:

- **One lifted card**: only the open folder is a card; closed folders are plain rows; top-level
  pages are full-size rows;
- **group labels** for long tracks, starting with this site's Maintain;
- the **scale ladder**: each sidebar level is a smaller body than the one above it, following
  the universe hierarchy the owner wants for the whole site;
- the rename to *Puncoz Verse* and the whole-site universe theme in a separate ADR (ADR-0028),
  after this one.

## Decision

1. **Each level of the sidebar is a smaller body than the one above it.** From the top:

   | Level | Body | Mark and type |
   |---|---|---|
   | The entry | **Planet** | The planet row: chip and the sun and moon (ADR-0018 decision 1), unchanged |
   | A version line | **Moon** | The moon picker (ADR-0013), unchanged |
   | A top-level page | **Landmark satellite** | An 8px satellite dot, the name in 14.5px Text 500, Frost Ink |
   | A group | **Belt** | A small label (11px Text 600, uppercase, 0.08em tracking, Steel Faint) on a dotted hairline running to the edge |
   | A folder | **Orbit** | A 12px ring, the name in 14px Text 500, the page count in 11.5px Data, Steel Faint, at the right |
   | A page in a folder | **Satellite** | A 6px hollow dot on the orbit's arc, the name in 13.5px Text |
   | The current page | **Probe** | A 9px teal dot with its glow, the row tinted with 10% of the ecosystem colour, Frost Ink 500 |

   Moons stay version lines only (DESIGN.md, *The Mark Means Rule*); a page is a satellite.

2. **Only the open orbit is a card.** A closed folder is a plain row: no border and no fill,
   its ring, name and count, and a chevron in Steel Faint. The open folder lifts into the one
   card in the sidebar: Graphite Panel, a hairline border, 12px corners; its ring fills with a
   planet (as the station glyph does today), and its satellites follow on their arc. When it
   holds the current page its border takes 45% of the ecosystem colour, as today. Opening a
   folder lifts it and drops the open one back to a row (ADR-0023, unchanged).

3. **Nested folders stay inside the card.** A folder inside the open orbit is a smaller orbit
   row (a 10px ring, 13.5px name). Opening it shows its satellites indented inside the same
   card; there is never a card inside a card. ADR-0023 holds at each level.

4. **Top-level pages are landmarks.** Pages directly in a track or line (the *Overview* item of
   ADR-0025, a track's own index listed in its `pages`, any other top-level page) sit above the
   first belt or orbit as full-size rows. The current one is the probe.

5. **Belts group orbits.** A Fumadocs separator in a `meta.json` is drawn as a belt (decision 1),
   with 18px above it. A track without separators shows no belts. This site's Maintain track
   gets four, in `content/docs/maintain/meta.json`:

   | Belt | Folders |
   |---|---|
   | Decisions | ADRs |
   | Design | Architecture, App flows, Product requirements, API, Data flow diagrams, Sequence diagrams, State machines, ERD |
   | Security | Access control, Security |
   | Templates | Templates |

   `PROJECT_AGENTS.md` tells consuming repositories they may group their own tracks the same
   way.

6. **A folder holding only its index is a plain orbit link.** It shows its ring and name, no
   count and no chevron, and opens its index page (today it shows a count of 0).

7. **The ADR constellation** (ADR-0019) is unchanged, inside the ADRs' card when it is open.

8. **Unchanged:** the planet row, the moon picker, the trajectory, the version notices, the
   pages' arc, one open orbit at each level (ADR-0023), the overview's sidebar (ADR-0025),
   keyboard behaviour (Fumadocs' collapsible trigger and `aria-expanded`), and the phone drawer,
   which shows the same sidebar. With reduced motion an orbit lifts and drops without animation.

## Options considered

| Concern | Option | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shape | A — one lifted card | Smallest change; the card says "you are in this orbit" | Closed folders lose their glyph-in-a-box look | Chosen (owner) |
| Shape | B — orbit rail: no cards, one continuous orbit line with every page and folder on it | Echoes the trajectory on the right | The biggest change; a long rail is hard to scan | Rejected (owner) |
| Shape | C — drill-in levels: one level at a time, ← back | Calm at every size | Hides the sibling folders; replaces ADR-0023 | Rejected (owner) |
| Marks | A — scale ladder: a body per level, sized to its rank | Follows the universe hierarchy; rank shows in size | One more mark (the ring) per closed row | Chosen (owner) |
| Marks | B — bodies only up top, plain type below | Quietest | Loses the theme below the planet row | Rejected (owner) |
| Groups | A — belts from `meta.json` separators | Uses Fumadocs' own separators; a content choice per track | Each track's maintainers place them | Chosen (owner) |
| Groups | B — one list | Nothing to maintain | Twelve folders in one run | Rejected (owner) |

## Consequences

**Positive**

- One card in the sidebar at a time, so the reader sees where they are at a glance.
- The two entry points, *Overview* and the track's own index, read as the most important rows.
- Long tracks scan by group.

**Negative / accepted trade-offs**

- A closed folder is quieter than today; its count and ring carry its weight.
- Belts need a separator per group in each track's `meta.json`; a track without them shows one
  list.
- The sidebar's look changes on every docs page at once.

## Content invariants

None touched: the change is to how the sidebar looks, and to the order labels of this site's
own Maintain track. Pages, URLs, per-page Markdown and `llms.txt` stay as they are (a
separator adds a bold label line to `llms.txt`, which keeps it readable).

## Security considerations

None — this change touches no data, no endpoint and no secret. It changes the sidebar's
presentation and one `meta.json`.
