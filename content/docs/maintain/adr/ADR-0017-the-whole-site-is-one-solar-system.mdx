## Context

The owner reviewed the home page built for ADR-0016 on 2026-11-01. In their words: the idea of
orbits and planets is good, but the site does not justify it. They asked for:

1. A footer that is more useful and different from a single credit line.
2. A different approach to Browse everything, and no "Browse everything" heading: there will be
   at most four or five packages and projects.
3. A redesigned private projects section, with sample projects and an empty state.
4. A clearer theme switch. The orbit-phase concept stays; the drawing is unclear.
5. The whole site reworked around the star, orbits and planets.

They then chose (2026-11-01):

- **Scope:** everything, docs pages included: home, header, theme switch, footer, package hero,
  version notices, 404, sidebar and table of contents.
- **Entries:** planet cards. Each package or project gets a card with its planet drawn in its
  ecosystem colour, its version lines as moons, a fact sheet and Use / Maintain links.
- **Private projects:** shown on the live site as real-looking projects, not marked as samples;
  their links may lead nowhere. The empty state is still designed.
- **Footer:** Horizon. A closing band where the star rises over a planet's curved horizon.

What the code and the installed packages settle:

- The metaphor already runs through the data: ecosystems are rings (`src/components/home/rings.ts`),
  entries are bodies, version lines are moons (ADR-0014). Only the home hero draws it; the cards,
  docs pages, footer and switch do not.
- **A custom theme switch needs no new dependency.** `fumadocs-ui/provider/base` exports
  `useTheme` (re-exported from next-themes; `package.json` `exports` lists `./provider/base`).
  Today's switch is Fumadocs' `ThemeSwitch` with its two icons hidden by CSS, so its drawing
  cannot show a sun and a terminator.
- **Fumadocs' notebook layout takes replacements for its slots.** The sidebar, TOC and page
  header are styled today through `src/app/site.css`; the version and track switches are this
  site's own components in `src/components/docs/`.
- **Cache Components stays as decided** (ADR-0016 decision 6). Nothing here reads runtime data.

## Decision

1. **One metaphor, stated once and used everywhere.** The star is the maker, Puncoz Nepal, at the
   centre of the system. Each ecosystem is an orbit. A package is a solid planet in its
   ecosystem's colour; a project is a ringed planet. Each version line is a moon: the latest
   release full, older releases crescents, `dev` a new moon drawn dashed. Use is a planet's day
   side; Maintain its night side. The hero's legend teaches this once; every later surface uses
   the same marks without explaining them again.

2. **The hero is the system.** Text on the left and the system on the right on desktop; the
   system above the text on phones. The star is labelled "Puncoz Nepal". Every planet is always
   labelled (the catalogue stays small), its moons circle it, and its night side faces away from
   the star. Pointing at a planet slows the system and shows its card; clicking opens its
   overview. The hero search button and legend stay (ADR-0016 decision 1).

3. **Planet cards replace Browse everything.** No section heading on screen (a visually hidden
   h2, "Packages and projects", keeps the outline). One card per public entry, in catalogue
   order, in a grid that fits two or three across on desktop and one on phones. A card has:
   - a sky panel drawing the planet with one orbit per version line and its moons, each labelled
     with the line's name (`v0.1`, `dev`); unreleased orbits dashed; a project shows its ring
     and no moons;
   - the name, linked to the overview (the whole card is the link target);
   - kind and ecosystem, the description;
   - a fact sheet: Latest, In development, Older lines, from the catalogue fields;
   - Use →, Maintain and Demo links, each only when that track or page exists.
   `#packages` and `#projects` keep working as header anchors: the first package card carries
   `id="packages"`, the first project card `id="projects"`.

4. **Private projects sit beyond an asteroid belt.** A scattered belt divides the public
   planets from an h2 "Private projects" and one line of copy. Private projects are drawn as
   night-side planets on dashed orbits with a lock mark, in a lighter, dashed card: name,
   description and a short meta line (client or internal, which tracks). Their names link to
   their future URL, which returns the site's 404 today. The data lives in code, in
   `src/components/home/private-projects.ts`, never in `content/`. With no private projects,
   the group shows an empty dashed orbit and "No private projects in orbit yet. They'll appear
   here once private documentation is set up." Everyone sees the group until the sign-in ADR
   (ADR-0016 decision 5 stands for who sees it).

   The private projects shown are **harbor-ops** ("Operations dashboard for a shipping
   client."), **fieldnote** ("Offline survey app for field teams.") and **tessera** ("Internal
   billing and invoicing service."). They are invented names chosen by the owner's instruction;
   they are not marked as samples on the page.

5. **Two sides of every planet** replaces "Two tracks, one place": Use as the day side and
   Maintain as the night side, split by a terminator drawn through a planet. Each side lists
   real pages of one featured entry with their line, as today's preview does.

6. **Every moon, released or not** replaces the version rails: one row per entry with two or
   more lines, its moons in order (older crescents, the latest full, `dev` new and dashed), each
   with its name and version. A three-item legend names the phases.

7. **Read it anywhere stays** (ADR-0016 decision 7) with the new card and panel styling.

8. **The Horizon footer, on every page.** A closing line, "Everything here orbits one maker:
   Puncoz Nepal" (linked to `https://puncoz.com`); below it the star rising behind a planet's
   curved horizon with a thin atmosphere rim in the accent colour; then, on the planet's surface,
   one link per public planet (its mark and name), and a row with Search `⌘K`, `llms.txt`,
   `llms-full.txt`, GitHub and "Back to orbit ↑". On docs pages the footer drops the scene and
   keeps the surface rows, so reading pages stay calm. The links wrap on phones.

9. **The theme switch is a small orrery** (supersedes ADR-0016 decision 8). A client component,
   `src/components/theme-switch.tsx`, using `useTheme` from `fumadocs-ui/provider/base`, draws
   an inline SVG in the 36px header button: a tilted orbit, a sun on it and a planet with a
   terminator. In light the sun stands in front of the planet and its face is fully lit (day
   side); in dark the sun moves behind the planet, smaller, and the planet shows a thin crescent
   (night side). The change animates for about half a second, and instantly under reduced motion.
   Its accessible name says what it does: "Switch to night side (dark theme)" or "Switch to day
   side (light theme)", and a tooltip says the same. Before hydration it renders the dark
   drawing (the site's default) with no layout shift.

10. **Docs pages keep the planet in view.** In the Fumadocs notebook layout:
    - the sidebar opens with a **planet chip** (mark, name, kind), a **day/night switch** for Use
      and Maintain (sun and moon marks drawn as SVG), and on Use a **moon picker** whose items
      show each line's phase mark;
    - the sidebar's link list and the TOC hang on a thin **orbit line**; the current page is a
      filled planet dot on that line;
    - an entry's overview opens with a **planet header**: kind, name, description, fact sheet and
      links on the left, its planet and labelled moons drawn on the right (below on phones);
    - **version notices** carry the moon of their line: crescent for an older release, a dashed
      new moon for `dev`;
    - page headings and body text are unchanged; the reading column stays calm.

11. **The 404 is "drifted out of orbit".** A planet that has left its dashed orbit, the heading
    "This page drifted out of orbit.", one line of help, the search button and "Back to the
    system →". It replaces Fumadocs' default not-found page for every path.

12. **The search dialog stays Fumadocs' own**, coloured by the existing tokens.

13. **Tokens.** One token is added, `--star` (light `#e08a00`, dark `#ffe2a8`) with
    `--star-glow`, and `--night` for a planet's unlit face. Everything else uses the existing
    tokens and fonts (ADR-0015). Every planet, moon, sun, lock and phase mark is drawn SVG; no
    emoji or text glyphs as icons.

## Options considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope A — everything, docs pages too | One world from home to the last docs page; the owner's ask | Largest change; touches the notebook layout's chrome | **Chosen** by the owner |
| Scope B — home page, header, switch and footer only | Smaller | Docs pages keep the old look; the metaphor stops at the home page | Rejected |
| Entries A — planet cards with moons and fact sheet | Uses the metaphor to carry real data (versions); suits 4–5 entries | Taller than rows; needs care to stay readable at one column | **Chosen** by the owner |
| Entries B — the hero system as the only index, no cards | Most striking | Hides facts behind hover; poor for keyboard and screen readers | Rejected |
| Entries C — keep ruled rows, no heading | Least work | Does not answer "the site does not justify the concept" | Rejected |
| Private A — invented projects on the live site, unmarked | What the owner asked for; shows the section's intended shape | Visitors read invented projects as real; dead links (see trade-offs) | **Chosen** by the owner |
| Private B — invented projects only in prototypes; empty state live | Nothing invented reaches the public site | The live section stays an empty state until the sign-in ADR | Rejected by the owner |
| Footer A — Horizon | A real ending; carries useful links (planets, llms, search, top) | A large decorative band | **Chosen** by the owner |
| Footer B — a compact link grid | Familiar, small | Generic; no part of the world | Rejected |
| Switch A — custom SVG orrery with `useTheme` | Can draw a sun and a terminator; no new dependency | A component to own instead of Fumadocs' | **Chosen** (recommended) |
| Switch B — restyle Fumadocs' `ThemeSwitch` with CSS only | No new component | CSS pseudo-elements cannot draw the terminator clearly; that is today's problem | Rejected |

## Consequences

**Positive**

- One idea explains the whole site: a reader who learns the legend on the home page can read
  versions and tracks on every docs page from the marks alone.
- The cards carry the facts the rows carried, plus the version lines, without a heading or a
  filter.
- The footer is useful: it links every planet, the Markdown routes, search and the top.
- The switch states its result in words and in the drawing.

**Negative / accepted trade-offs**

- **Invented private projects on the live site.** harbor-ops, fieldnote and tessera are not real
  projects, but the page presents them as real. Visitors may ask about them, and their links
  return the 404. This departs from `PRODUCT.md`'s "no invented data" stance and from ADR-0016's
  "no private project, name or count exists on the site". The owner accepts it; replacing the
  list is a code change in one file.
- More SVG on every page. The drawings are small, inline and static except the hero canvas.
- A custom theme switch to maintain, and the notebook sidebar's chrome to keep working across
  Fumadocs upgrades.
- The 404 becomes this site's own page.

## Content invariants

- **1, Private stays private.** Kept in substance: no private documentation exists, and the
  invented private projects are names and one-line descriptions in a component file, never in
  `content/`, the search index, `static.json`, `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, per-page Markdown or
  Open Graph images. They are not real projects, so nothing private is disclosed. Real private
  project names must never be put in that file: that waits for the private-documentation ADR.
- **3, version on every page.** Strengthened: the moon picker and notices show the line and its
  version.
- **5, readable as Markdown.** Unchanged: the redesign changes HTML chrome only.
- **2 and 4** are untouched.

## Security considerations

- **Data handled:** public catalogue fields (unchanged) and three invented private project
  entries, classified public. No secret, environment variable or endpoint is added.
- **Private to public:** the invented list is the one place a private project name could ever be
  typed into code. The risk is that someone later replaces an invented name with a real client
  name. Mitigation: the file states that only invented entries belong there until the
  private-documentation ADR, and `security/threat-model.mdx` records the risk.
- **Authorization:** none; everyone sees the same page (ADR-0016 decision 5).
- **What leaves the system:** nothing new. The invented entries are not sent to Algolia.
- **Input:** none new. The 404 renders no part of the requested path.
- **Residual risk accepted:** visitors may take the invented private projects as real.
