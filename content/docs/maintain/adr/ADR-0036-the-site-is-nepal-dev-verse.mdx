## Context

- The owner wants to publish this site as a documentation platform for the packages and projects of
  Nepalese developers all over the world, under the name **Nepal Dev Verse**, and has supplied the
  nepal-dev.com logo in `temp/logo.svg`. They asked for references to Puncoz Nepal to become
  nepal-dev.com, with a footer credit to nepal-dev.com and "made with ❤️ by Puncoz Nepal".
- The name appears through `appName` in `src/lib/shared.ts`. The maker appears through `owner` in the
  same file: the star's label, the footer's credit, the page metadata and the JSON-LD.
- What was measured of the logo:
  - It is 1.6 MB of SVG, 551 paths, 1395 by 1127: a mountain range with circuit traces, Nepal's flag on
    the summit and the wordmark *nepal-dev.com*.
  - On the site's dark ground (`#0a0c10`, the default theme) the navy *nepal* and *.com*, the cursor
    and the mountains' outline nearly vanish.
  - At 48px the wordmark cannot be read; at 16px the artwork is a smudge.
- Nepal's flag holds a moon above a sun. The site's sidebar already uses a sun for Use and a moon for
  Maintain, and its version lines are moons (ADR-0017), so the flag sits well in this site's own language.
  [ADR-0037](./ADR-0037-the-horizon-is-the-himalaya.mdx) builds on that.
- Today the content is this site's own documentation only, and only the owner reviews and commits what
  reaches `content/` (ADR-0010).

## Decision

### The name

1. **The site is Nepal Dev Verse.** `appName` becomes *Nepal Dev Verse*, wherever it reaches today: the
   wordmark, titles, share images, the manifest, and the heading of `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`.
2. **This site's own docs are Nepal Dev Verse Docs.** The project with the slug `docs` takes that title in
   its `meta.json` and overview. The slug and every URL stay: `/docs`, `/docs/maintain/…`.

### What it says it is

3. **The home line reads: "Every package and project by Nepalese developers all over the world,
   documented to the version."** It is the hero's heading, with *documented to the version.* as its
   emphasis, the home share image's headline, set smaller to fit, and the manifest's description.
4. **The home page's title is `Nepal Dev Verse — packages and projects by Nepalese developers`.** The
   full line is too long for a search result.
5. **The star is nepal-dev.com.** ADR-0017's metaphor stands, with the community in the maker's place: the
   star on the home page is labelled *nepal-dev.com*, and the Verse view's plain sentence names it.

### The logo

6. **The original is kept, unchanged, at `assets/nepal-dev-logo.svg`,** as the master. It is not served and
   not published. The owner removes `temp/`.
7. **A small summit-and-flag mark is redrawn from it** for everywhere the site shows a brand: about 2 KB
   of inline SVG in `src/components/brand-mark.tsx`, with a central peak, two shoulders, the snow, the
   logo's red face and one teal trace, all flying the flag.
   - **The flag keeps its own colours in both themes, and is never restyled.** Its shape, the moon above
     the sun, comes from the logo.
   - **The rock follows the theme:** the logo's navy on the light ground, a lifted blue from the logo's
     palette on the dark one, with at least 3:1 against the ground.
   - **It stands in** the site header, `src/app/icon.svg`, `src/app/apple-icon.tsx`, the share images and
     the footer's credit. At 16px the trace is left out and the flag simplified, so the favicon stays readable.
8. **The Verse mark stays as a place, not a brand:** only in the scale strip, for the Verse level.

### The credit and the references

9. **The footer's credit reads: "Part of nepal-dev.com. Made with ❤️ by Puncoz Nepal."**, with the mark
   before it. *nepal-dev.com* links to `https://nepal-dev.com` and *Puncoz Nepal* to `https://puncoz.com`.
10. **Page metadata and JSON-LD name nepal-dev.com:** `authors`, `creator` and `publisher`, `article:author`,
    and an `Organization` with its URL in place of the owner's `Person`, with no `sameAs` because no
    profile is known. Per-entry authors wait for the contribution ADR (decision 13).
11. **The public address is `https://docs.nepal-dev.com`,** still from `SITE_URL`; ADR-0035's rules for it
    are unchanged. The owner adds the DNS record and sets the variable.

### What stays

12. **Puncoz Nepal stays** in the footer's credit, as `@puncoz` in ADRs (ADR-0020), and as *the owner* in
    the working documents. Approved ADRs keep the names they were written with. The repository and package
    names, the GitHub link (`puncoz-official/docs`), the Algolia index and the environment variables are
    identifiers and do not change here.
13. **This is the identity, not the platform's workings.** How other developers contribute, who reviews
    them, and showing who built each entry all wait for their own ADR. Until then ADR-0010 stands and the
    owner remains the only reviewer. The private projects beyond the belt stay as they are, invented, by
    the owner's choice.

## Consequences

**Positive**

- The site says what it is for, and looks like part of nepal-dev.com, on every page and every shared link.
- The next step, inviting other developers, starts from a name and an identity that already fit.

**Negative / accepted trade-offs**

- **The words go ahead of the content:** the home page says "by Nepalese developers" while one project,
  this site, is documented. It also shows invented private projects under that name.
- The long home line takes more of the first screen, most of all on phones. It is measured at 320px and
  390px during implementation.
- The original logo adds 1.6 MB to the repository, and so to every consuming submodule's checkout.
- The repository and GitHub names keep *puncoz* until the owner renames them.
- The full logo appears nowhere on the site itself; only the mark drawn from it does.
- Nothing has been said here about whether `https://nepal-dev.com` is live. The owner confirms it before
  publishing, because the credit links there.

## Content invariants

None is departed from. §6.1 stands: nothing private goes into `content/`, and the belt's names stay
invented. No page moves, so §6.5's Markdown surfaces keep working; they take the new name. The search index
takes it on the next publish, which only the owner runs.

## Security considerations

- **No new endpoint, secret or third party.** `SITE_URL` changes value, not handling, and stays public.
- **Two external links** in the credit, both fixed, with `rel="noreferrer noopener"`.
- **The logo master** is not served, so it adds no surface. It was read before use: paths and masks only, with
  no script, external reference or embedded raster image. The mark is hand-drawn and inline.
- **A community name raises the stakes of §6.1.** The invented private projects could be read as real
  community projects; `private-projects.ts`'s rule stands, and the contribution ADR must revisit it.

What the build added or changed from the above, and what was measured:

- `src/components/brand-mark.tsx` redraws the flag by hand to the logo's proportions rather than using its paths: the logo
  is 1.6 MB and 551 paths, with no script, external resource or raster, and its flag alone is 83 auto-traced paths.
  `src/lib/flag.ts` and `src/components/nepal-flag.tsx` hold the flag, shared with ADR-0037's Horizon.
- The hero's line takes `max-width: 19ch` and a smaller minimum, `clamp(1.85rem, 1.5rem + 2.1vw, 3.4rem)`: six lines at
  320px, five at 390px, three at 860px, four on a wide screen, with no sideways scroll. The share image's headline is 54px.
- In Chrome the longer project name crossed the empty orbits' names on a phone, so `orbit-map.tsx` draws a planet's name
  with a halo in the ground colour. With many planets on a phone the names still crowd each other as they turn; that is
  ADR-0032's system at that width and was not changed here.
- `src/lib/site-name.test.ts` allows one exception to the old names, a row quoting ADR-0028's approved title.
- `README.md`'s list of decisions stopped at ADR-0028, so ADR-0029 to ADR-0037 were added to it.

**For the owner after approval:** set `SITE_URL=https://docs.nepal-dev.com` in `.env.local` and on the host, add
the DNS record, confirm `https://nepal-dev.com` is live, and remove `temp/`.
