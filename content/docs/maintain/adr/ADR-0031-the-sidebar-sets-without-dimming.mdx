## Context

- Under ADR-0030 decision 1, when the Horizon footer rises over a docs page, the sidebar and the
  trajectory **set**: they drop up to 24px and dim to 55%, in step with the scroll
  (`@keyframes horizon-set` in `src/app/site.css`).
- What stays above the horizon still works: its links can be read, hovered and clicked.
- A light-theme contrast audit, run in Chrome against `bunx next build` on
  `/docs`, found that at 55% this text falls below WCAG AA's 4.5:1 on Graphite Ground
  (`#f6f7f9`). The ratios below are computed from the tokens in `src/app/global.css`:

  | Text | Full | 85% | 75% | 55% |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Ink | 17.8:1 | 11.9:1 | 8.3:1 | 4.1:1 |
  | Muted | 6.7:1 | 4.7:1 | 3.7:1 | 2.5:1 |
  | Steel Faint (separators, counts, the trajectory) | 5.2:1 | 3.8:1 | 3.2:1 | 2.2:1 |
  | Teal (the open station, the current page) | 4.9:1 | 3.8:1 | 3.2:1 | 2.3:1 |

- No dim level keeps every text colour at 4.5:1; even 85% fails Steel Faint and Teal.
- The dusk edge already does the setting's visual work: what runs below the horizon fades into
  the ground over 96px and is hidden.
- The owner chose, with options: drop only, no dimming.

## Decision

1. **The sidebar and the trajectory set by dropping, not by dimming.** As the Horizon enters, they
   drop up to 24px in step with the scroll, as now, and stay at full opacity. What runs below the
   horizon still sinks into the dusk edge and is hidden. In both themes.
2. **Unchanged from ADR-0030:**
   - the scroll range (entry 0% to 60%), the 768px minimum and the reduced-motion and
     no-support fallbacks;
   - the footer's stacking, its dusk edge and everything in the scene.

## Options considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — Drop only, no dimming | Every text colour keeps its contrast above the horizon; the dusk edge still does the setting | The sidebar no longer looks like it fades as it sets | Chosen (owner) |
| B — Dim only in dark | Keeps the fade where it reads as dusk | Dark keeps the same failure, measured against darker tokens; two behaviours to keep | Rejected |
| C — Dim less (75% or 85%) | A softer fade | Still under 4.5:1 for Steel Faint and Teal at any level that shows | Rejected |
| D — Keep 55% | No change | Readable, clickable text below AA at the end of every docs page | Rejected |

## Consequences

**Positive**

- The part of the sidebar and trajectory still above the horizon keeps the contrast every other
  text on the site has (DESIGN.md: every text colour 4.5:1 or more).
- One property fewer animates on the sidebar and the trajectory.

**Negative / accepted trade-offs**

- The setting is a drop and the dusk edge only; nothing fades as it sinks except where the dusk
  edge covers it.

## Content invariants

None is touched. No content, URL or Markdown surface changes.

## Security considerations

None. This change touches no data, no endpoint and no secret.

## Implementation notes

- [x] `src/app/site.css`: `@keyframes horizon-set` keeps `translate: 0 24px` and drops its
  `opacity`.
- [x] Tests: the setting is CSS on Fumadocs' layout and is not rendered in a unit test
  (ADR-0021). `src/components/horizon-footer.test.tsx` reads the keyframes from `site.css`
  instead: they drop 24px and set no opacity (it failed before the change). The contrast of
  the sidebar's text at full opacity was measured in the light-theme audit that led to this ADR
  (the table above). Checked in Chrome after the change, in the light theme at the end of a
  docs page: the sidebar and the trajectory stand at opacity 1, dropped 24px; a full contrast
  scan was not re-run.
- [x] `bun run test`, `bun run types:check` and `bunx next build` pass; `bun run lint` is reported
  while it stays blocked.
